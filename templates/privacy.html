About your data.

CineCompass uses first-party, optional analytics to understand which features people use.

Anonymous usage measurement

When you choose Allow, this site counts successful page views, autocomplete requests and review analyses. A random browser cookie helps estimate distinct visitors. The database stores a keyed digest of that random identifier, a UTC date and aggregate counters for fixed page categories.

Analytics does not store IP addresses, names, account IDs, search queries, movie IDs, review text or referring URLs. It does not send analytics to a third-party provider. The owner’s visits are excluded while signed in as the configured owner. Recognised bots are filtered, but these estimates cannot identify every bot or every human.

The visitor cookie lasts 30 days. Your allow/decline preference lasts one year. Usage records older than 90 UTC calendar days are removed on the next counted request or scheduled cleanup. On an idle site, records remain until cleanup runs. Declining stops future measurement and deletes the visitor cookie; previously collected aggregates remain until cleanup.

Browsers sending Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control are not counted, even if Allow was selected earlier. Different browsers, cleared cookies and expired cookies may count the same person again. Visitors who decline are not included, so totals are not the number of all people using the site.

Accounts and saved content

Account usernames, password hashes, watchlists and genre preferences are stored separately to provide account features. Reviews are saved to your account only when you select Save to my history, and can be deleted from My library. Total account counts in the owner dashboard include all registered accounts regardless of analytics preference; individual account details are not displayed there.

Movie data and service operation

Movie searches and movie IDs are sent to TMDB to retrieve results; movie artwork is loaded from TMDB’s image servers. Review sentiment analysis runs on this application’s server. Session, CSRF and optional remember-me cookies support sign-in and form protection independently of analytics. The hosting provider and application server may retain operational access/error logs, including request information; the analytics controls do not control those logs.
